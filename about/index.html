Who We Are

Individual properties, evaluated on the opportunities they present

Scot Kenney Acquisitions is guided by an entrepreneurial approach to identifying real estate opportunities and considering practical ways to improve their usefulness, appeal, and financial performance. Rather than relying on rigid acquisition categories, the company evaluates individual properties and the specific opportunities they present.

Our interests range from conventional income-producing assets to distinctive waterfront and resort properties and new residential concepts. We recognize that successful execution depends on careful underwriting, appropriate financing, local market knowledge, and collaboration with qualified professionals.

We seek productive relationships with property owners, commercial brokers, family offices, established capital firms, developers, and experienced operating organizations.
