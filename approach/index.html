Approach

An Opportunity-Driven Acquisition Philosophy

Illustrative imagery

We pursue opportunities where the acquisition basis, property characteristics, and a practical operating or development plan create a compelling business case. We are not restricted by a minimum or maximum number of apartment units, a single property category, or a one-size-fits-all financing structure.

Our Process

Five disciplined steps

  1. Identify

    Source properties and situations where pricing, location, management, design, or unrealized potential warrant closer study.

  2. Underwrite

    Examine current income, realistic expenses, capital requirements, financing, regulations, and downside scenarios.

  3. Structure

    Explore an appropriate acquisition and partnership structure for the specific property.

  4. Execute

    Coordinate qualified local professionals and operating partners to implement the business plan.

  5. Reassess

    Monitor results and consider refinancing, long-term ownership, or disposition as circumstances warrant.

Partnering Philosophy

Complementary relationships

We value complementary relationships with experienced, well-capitalized organizations. Depending on the transaction, a capital partner may provide equity, financing resources, operating capabilities, development expertise, or a combination of these. Roles and economics are negotiated separately for each transaction.
