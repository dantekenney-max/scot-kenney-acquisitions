Waterfront Real Estate & Marinas

Waterfront apartments, marina assets, boat slips, and related properties may offer distinctive combinations of location, income, and repositioning possibilities. Every opportunity must be evaluated for local permitting, insurance, dockage rights, operating costs, and demand.

Distinctive RV & Motorcoach Destinations

We evaluate distinctive RV and motorcoach destinations where design, landscaping, hospitality, and amenities could create a compelling guest experience and additional revenue opportunities. As one example of our founder’s creative approach, Scot Kenney conceived a luxury motorcoach resort with a dramatic three-story black-glass clubhouse, private-site amenities, dining, and cultural events; an architect prepared an illustration of his concept. This was a design concept, not a completed development.
